Part 1 — Privacy Policy

What we collect

Contact-form leads: your name and phone number (+91), used only to call you back. Member accounts: your name, email and a hashed password — never stored in plain text. Assessment answers: age, height, current and target weight, goals, experience, training days and diet preference, used to build your plan.

Until our backend launches, account and assessment data lives only in your browser's local storage on your own device. Leads you submit are stored the same way until the backend endpoint is connected.

How we use it

To call you back, to open and manage your membership, to personalise your training and nutrition templates, and to operate the club. That's the complete list.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we run no ad-tracking or analytics of any kind on this site today.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay on their secure pages. Your card, UPI or banking details never touch our website or servers — Razorpay's own privacy policy governs that data.

Your choices

You can clear your local account data anytime by signing out and clearing site data. To have us delete or correct anything we hold (e.g. a lead), email us at the address above and we'll confirm within 72 hours.

Part 2 — Membership Terms

Memberships & payments

Durations are 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, prepaid in full via Razorpay. Your membership activates at payment confirmation; your first session is set up with a coach within 24 hours. Pause and transfer requests are handled case-by-case at the front desk or by email.

Refunds

Memberships cancelled within 7 days of purchase and before your first session are refunded in full to the original payment method. After 7 days, refunds are pro-rata for unused full months only.

Health & training

The assessment-generated training weeks and diet plans are general education templates — not medical advice. Consult your physician before starting, especially if you have existing conditions or injuries. You train at your own risk and agree to load and intensity choices appropriate to your ability.

Conduct

Respect the room: re-rack weights, sanitize equipment, no harassment of members or staff. Repeated violations may end a membership with a pro-rata refund.

Changes & governing

We may update these terms; the version on this page always applies at first reading on renewal. Governed by the laws of India; courts at Kharar, Punjab.
