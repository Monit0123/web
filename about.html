Our philosophy

Train with
purpose.

ONYX started with a simple observation: most gyms sell access. Rows of machines, a swipe card, and good luck. We think people deserve more than access — they deserve direction.

Every session at ONYX is coached, every program is progressive, and every member is known by name. We measure success not in check-ins, but in first pull-ups, first 5Ks, and the quiet confidence of people who trust their bodies again.

That is what “harder to break” really means to us — not just stronger muscles, but sturdier lives.

What we stand for

Principles that
never rest.

01

Coaching first

Every session is led, not left to chance. You will never have to guess what to do next.

02

Consistency over intensity

We program for years, not weeks. Progress you can keep beats progress you can post.

03

Community as equipment

The room raises your bar. Training alongside people who show up makes you someone who shows up.

04

Recovery is training

Rest is where the work lands. Mobility and recovery are programmed in, never bolted on.
