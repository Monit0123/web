Before you ask

The questions
we get most.

How is PT different from coached classes?

Classes follow the group program; PT is programmed only for you — your goals, injuries, schedule and pace, with undivided attention every rep.

How often should I do PT?

Most clients do 2–3 sessions a week plus independent training. Your coach will recommend a frequency after your trial.

Can I choose my trainer?

Yes. Meet the team on our trainers page or ask the front desk to match you by goal and schedule.

What does personal training cost?

Longer memberships include 1–4 personal sessions. For dedicated 1-on-1 packs, message us on WhatsApp for current rates.
