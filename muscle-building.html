I'm a beginner. Will I still grow?

Beginners grow fastest of all — new lifters often add visible size in 8–12 weeks on our coached starter blocks.

Do I need supplements?

No. Food first, always. If your protein falls short, whey from our shelf is convenient — never mandatory.

How many days a week should I train?

Four is the sweet spot for hypertrophy: two upper, two lower. Three full-body days works if you're busy.
