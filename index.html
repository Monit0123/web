Before you ask

The questions
we get most.

Can I try ONYX before I join?

Yes. Your first class is free, no card needed — book it above or call the front desk. If you would rather train solo first, a day pass is ₹299 and includes full floor access.

I have never trained before. Is this for me?

Especially for you. Every session is coached and scaled on the spot, and your first week includes a one-to-one onboarding where we set your starting loads. Roughly a third of our members had never touched a barbell before joining.

Do I need to book classes in advance?

Classes are capped at 14 so we recommend booking, but walk-ins are welcome whenever there is space. Open-floor hours never need a booking.

What does membership actually include?

All four plans include unlimited gym-floor access, every coached class on the timetable, the recovery zone, lockers and showers. The only difference between plans is how long you commit for — longer terms cost less per month.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership?

You can freeze any plan for up to 30 days a year at no cost. Cancellations and refunds follow the policy set out in our terms.

Is there parking, and what should I bring?

Free parking sits directly outside on Palm Meadows NH. Bring shoes you can lift in, a towel and a water bottle — we supply everything else, including chalk.
